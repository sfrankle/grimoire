# Field Notes rules

## What goes to the hub

- Settled facts about the pottery studio: kiln schedules, glaze recipes, studio rates, supplier contacts.
- Decisions that are made, with the date and the reason.

## What goes elsewhere

- Drafts and half-formed ideas stay in `~/notes/drafts`.
- A client's own files stay in that client's folder.

## When to suggest a save

- After a decision is settled and nobody is still debating it.
- When a fact comes up twice in different sessions.
- Suggest in one line and wait for a yes.
