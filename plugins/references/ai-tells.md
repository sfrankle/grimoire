# Current List of AI Tells (Stylistic, Syntactical, and Formatting Markers)
---

## 1. Punctuation & Formatting Tells

* **The Load-Bearing Em-Dash (`—`):** Models use em-dashes at a frequency far higher than average human writers—often multiple times per paragraph—to introduce parenthetical explanations, emphasis, or concluding thoughts.
* **Rule-of-Three Formatting:** AI heavily favors triads, routinely outputting lists, examples, or adjectives in groupings of three.
* **Emoji Bullet Points:** Using emojis (e.g., 🚀, 💡, 🎯, ✨) as bullet point icons rather than standard typographic bullets.
* **Boldface Overkill:** Bolding every lead-in phrase, key term, or header inside short paragraphs to force scannability.
* **Over-reliance on Colons:** Using colons to introduce nearly every explanatory clause or list item.

---

## 2. Syntactical & Structural Patterns

* **The Pivot / Contrast Formula:**
  * *"It's not just X; it's Y."*
  * *"Not only does X do Y, but it also Z."*
  * *"It’s about more than just [Concept A]—it’s about [Concept B]."*
* **Rhetorical Conversational Hooks:** Starting paragraphs or sections with forced conversational hooks:
  * *"Here’s the thing:"*
  * *"Honestly? ..."*
  * *"Sound familiar?"*
* **Symmetrical Paragraphs ("The Metronome"):** Paragraphs that are uniformly 3–4 sentences long, creating an unnaturally smooth and predictable reading cadence.
* **Ending Platitudes & Synthesized Summaries:** Concluding sections that wrap up with grand, balanced, or pseudo-profound truisms:
  * *"In a rapidly changing world, X is more important than ever."*
  * *"Ultimately, X remains a journey, not a destination."*
* **Over-structured Headers:** Every subsection having a neat, title-cased heading, even for short or casual explanations.

## 3. Vocabulary "Buzzwords"

Certain high-probability tokens appear far more frequently in AI-generated text than in standard human prose:

| Category | High-Frequency AI Vocabulary |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Verbs & Actions** | *Delve, Navigate, Foster, Harness, Unlock, Unleash, Elevate, Spearhead, Safeguard* |
| **Nouns & Metaphors** | *Tapestry, Landscape, Realm, Beacon, Cornerstone, Paradigm, Testament ("a testament to...")* |
| **Adjectives & Adverbs** | *Crucial, Vital, Paramount, Multi-faceted, Intricate, Transformative, Ever-evolving, Seamless* |

---

## 4. Tone & Stance Tells

* **The Neutral Middle Ground:** AI rarely takes an eccentric, idiosyncratic, or strongly biased stance without prompting. It naturally hedges and defaults to balanced nuance (*"While X offers Y, it is important to consider Z"*).
* **Absence of Tangential Specificity:** Human writing frequently includes irrelevant personal anecdotes, regional slang, or random off-topic observations. AI writing stays strictly focused on the core prompt logic.
* **Hyper-Clean Transitions:** Paragraphs seamlessly bridged by traditional transition words (*"Furthermore," "Moreover," "In contrast," "Consequently," "Ultimately"*) without abrupt context jumps.
